Arcane

“Long before the world forgot its magic, the forest learned to keep it.”

In the forgotten reaches of Hushwood, something ancient stirs beneath the trees.

Once, magic flowed freely through this land. Then came the silence and the old paths vanished into mist. Now, strange lights flicker between the branches, and forgotten forces begin to awaken.

You have found your way here for a reason.

The forest does not answer words. It answers to intent and the will to shape what lies unseen.

Raise your hands. The woods are watching.

Your story begins now

Know your magic

Seven spells. One pair of hands.

Each spell has its own move. Your live bindings are shown below.

CASTING

Follow the hand marker to aim. Hold hand signs until their charge fills; sweeps and circles cast as soon as the motion is recognized. For Arcane Bow, point, pinch with your other hand, pull, then release.

Hold your ground Your view stays fixed, leaving both hands free to cast. Eight spirits approach after “Awaken the wood”. Watch their health and attack wind-up; freeze them to interrupt, or use Mend and Aegis to survive. Spells are gesture-controlled—no movement or number-key casting.
