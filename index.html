ARcane

Cast spells with your bare hands.

Hold a hand up to your webcam. Shape a sign, trace a circle, sweep an arc, and magic answers in another world.

  • Sit about an arm’s length from the screen.
  • Put a light in front of you rather than behind you.
  • Use one hand or both, palms toward the camera.

Your browser will ask to use the camera.

The camera only reads your hand. Its picture is never shown, saved or sent anywhere.
