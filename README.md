### Magic was never meant to be bound to a button.

**Arcane is a gesture-controlled fantasy experience where your hands become your spellbook.**

Make a fist. Draw a circle in the air. Raise your palm against the dark.

Through your webcam, ARcane transforms your movements into magic, bringing spells to life in a strange, liminal world caught somewhere between a dream and a forgotten memory.

## 🌲 Enter the World of Hushwood

You wake on the floor of a dark wood with no memory of the way in. Something is coming through the trees.

Hushwood was bright once. Its lights were taken, and the creatures that remain have grown strange. Fight your way from the Hushed Edge to the Unlit Throne, learn what silenced the wood, and give it back its colours.

- **A journey of 40 levels** across four chapters, each level three waves that grow harder.
- **Eight bosses.** A sub-boss comes alone after the last wave of each chapter's ninth level; a ruler waits at the tenth, introduced by its own cutscene.
- **Nineteen spells.** Eleven from the first night, and eight legendary spells taken from the bosses who rule the wood.
- **A story told in scenes,** played live in the forest itself, never on video.
- **Creatures with real bodies** that walk, side-step, turn, charge, fly and coil, each with attacks you learn to read.

## 🪄 Play Arcane

**The easiest way to enter Hushwood is right in your browser.**

### [ Enter Hushwood](https://ar-cane-nine.vercel.app/)

Open the experience, allow webcam access, and use your hands to cast magic.

🌐 No installation required — just a webcam and a modern browser.

## 💻 Run Locally

Want to explore the code or run ARcane on your own machine?

**Requirements:** Node.js, npm, and a webcam.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/rishabh4884/ARcane.git
cd ARcane
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL provided by Vite and allow camera access.

## ⚙️ How It Works

- 👁️ **MediaPipe** — Detects hand landmarks.
- 🖐️ **Gesture Recognition** — Interprets hand poses and movements.
- 🌌 **Three.js** — Brings the world and magical effects to life.
