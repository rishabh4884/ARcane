### Magic was never meant to be bound to a button.

**ARcane is a gesture-controlled fantasy experience where your hands become your spellbook.**

Make a fist. Draw a circle in the air. Raise your palm against the dark.

Through your webcam, ARcane transforms your movements into magic, bringing spells to life in a strange, liminal world caught between dreams and forgotten memories.

## ✦ Enter the World of Hushwood

A forest suspended between the familiar and the otherworldly. Where shadows seem to breathe, ancient magic stirs, and the world feels alive with secrets.

Hushwood is ARcane's liminal fantasy realm — a world of ethereal light, drifting particles, and magic waiting to be discovered.

**Just you, your hands, and Hushwood.**

## Play ARcane

**The easiest way to enter Hushwood is right in your browser.**

### [✦ Enter Hushwood](https://ar-cane-nine.vercel.app/)

Open the experience, allow webcam access, and use your hands to cast magic.

No installation required — just a webcam and a modern browser.

## Run Locally

Want to explore the code or run ARcane on your own machine?

**Requirements:** Node.js, npm, and a webcam.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/rishabh4884/ARcane.git
cd ARcane
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL provided by Vite and allow camera access.

## How It Works

- **MediaPipe** — Detects hand landmarks.
- **Gesture Recognition** — Interprets hand poses and movements.
- **Three.js** — Brings the world and magical effects to life.
